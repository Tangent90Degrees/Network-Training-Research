# Problem Settings

**Problem**: we study the problem of accelerating the training process in a deep learning model in computer vision area.

**Context and gaps**: There are many models and fields in CV. Classification aims to classify an image into a learned class using labeled or unlabeled data, while reconstruction aims at reconstructing the masked area of an image. Several novel structures, such as CNN and ViT, have been proposed, demonstrating great ability and potential in solving such problems. However, we find that the training process for some programs lacks efficiency, especially in reconstruction tasks and ViT-based models. We found that [reference] has proposed several novel methods for the acceleration of training in CNN based classification models. Therefore, we investigated into the problem that whether leveraging this methods and also have some effects in the training of a ViT or a CNN-based reconstruction model.
